# M9 local fake reconciliation job (default OFF)

This is an **explicitly invoked local test-only** worker. Construct `marketplace.ReconciliationJob{Enabled:true,Accounting:marketplace.FakeAccounting{DB:db,Enabled:true,FeeBPS:...},Lease:20*time.Second,Backoff:time.Second,MaxAttempts:3}` and call `Enqueue(ctx,"unique-job-id")`, then `Tick(ctx)` from a trusted, bounded operator harness. **No background loop, cron registration, HTTP endpoint, deployment or runtime startup wiring exists**; the zero-value Enabled flag denies work. A unique job ID is required for each later accounting pass, including refunds.

`fake_reconciliation_jobs` holds a due time, unique ID, attempts, random lease token and expiry, state, error category and inserted-row count. Each tick claims at most one due record through a conditional SQL update in a transaction. A crashed lease may be reclaimed after expiry. The job checks the lease before invoking existing `FakeAccounting.RunReconciliationJob`, then conditionally acknowledges using its token and unexpired lease. A stale worker cannot finalize another worker's job. Because the existing fake accounting commits its **own** transaction, a lease expiring mid-accounting can permit a second execution: immutable ledger uniqueness and transactional reconciliation make this safe for fake accounting, but the scheduler does not guarantee exactly-once invocation. Do not use this design for non-idempotent provider side effects.

Failures use capped exponential backoff (configured base 1 second–1 minute; maximum 1 hour), at most 1–10 attempts, then a dead state. Missing sale snapshots remain a hard reconciliation error (with count), **never** inferred from mutable listings; failure writes no partial ledger. Refund reversals negate the original recorded sale fee/gross/net, even when FeeBPS changed. No real settlement, payments or payouts occur. No automatic retry loop runs unless a trusted caller explicitly calls Tick again.

The job supports local concurrency and crash-recovery tests; remote/multi-process operating environments and payment-provider settlement have not been certified. Internal engineering defaults only; no legal or owner release approval is implied.
