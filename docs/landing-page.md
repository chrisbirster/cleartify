## Visual direction

- playful consumer product, not enterprise software;
- large `Text Clear.` hero;
- meme line: `Stop raw-dogging your life.`;
- stickers/doodles such as `no app lol`, `literally just text it`, and `main character support`;
- phone/text conversation as the visual proof of the product;
- soft pastel cards and approachable icons;
- protocols explained with everyday examples rather than workflow jargon;
- privacy/trust language stays calm and explicit;
- final CTA collects name, email and mobile number.

## Product copy hierarchy

1. **Text Clear. It'll figure it out.** — durable product promise.
2. **Text Clear. Stop raw-dogging your life.** — meme-friendly acquisition headline.
3. **Less to juggle. More you.** — emotional benefit.
4. **Clearly.** — recurring punchline/brand device.

The landing page may rotate acquisition copy over time without changing Clear's calmer in-product personality.
